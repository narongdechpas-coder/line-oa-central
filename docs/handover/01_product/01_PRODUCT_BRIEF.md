# 01 — Product Brief

## Product
**Jacky Central Communication & Case Management Platform**

## Product category
Centralized enterprise communication and case orchestration platform.

## Problem statement
Departments operate separate LINE Official Accounts. Conversations and customer context are fragmented, ownership can be unclear, handoffs are difficult to track, and management has limited visibility into workload, escalation and service levels.

## Product objective
Provide one operational workspace to oversee multiple LINE OA channels while retaining channel-specific customer identity and enabling internal work orchestration across departments.

## Target users
- Super Admin / System Admin
- Department Admin
- Manager
- Supervisor
- PIC / Agent
- Auditor

## Value proposition
- One operational view across multiple LINE OA accounts.
- Clear case ownership and assignment history.
- PIC recommendations based on skills, workload, availability and experience.
- Structured internal escalation and task handoff.
- SLA visibility and management reporting.
- Role- and resource-scoped access control.

## Demo success criteria
A stakeholder can understand the product concept and complete one end-to-end customer service scenario in under five minutes without explanation.

## Product principles
1. Multiple LINE OA accounts remain independent channels.
2. Customer identity is scoped to the source OA; do not auto-merge across OAs.
3. Every conversation remains bound to its original channel.
4. Case origin channel and original customer are immutable.
5. Escalation is an internal workflow only.
6. Replies always use the conversation's original channel.
7. PIC recommendations are advisory; an authorized manager confirms or overrides.
8. Authority means access control, not business approval authority.
9. All important ownership and status changes should be visible in a timeline.
